Participation

When you create or join a Buddy Session, you voluntarily share a display name and task title with your session partner. You are responsible for the information you choose to share.

Conduct

When using Buddy Sessions, you agree to:

  • Use the feature for its intended purpose of collaborative focus
  • Not share offensive, inappropriate, or harmful content through display names or task titles
  • Not attempt to disrupt or interfere with other users' sessions
  • Not attempt to exploit the session code system

Data Handling

Buddy session data is stored temporarily on Firebase servers and is automatically deleted within 24 hours. During an active session, your partner can see your display name, task title, focus status, and distraction metrics.
