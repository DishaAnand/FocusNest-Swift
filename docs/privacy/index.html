Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 18, 2026

FocusHaven is a productivity application built by Disha Anand. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you use FocusHaven.

📱 Information Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device and synced across your own devices via your personal iCloud account. The developer does not have access to this data.

  • Focus session history including duration, completion status, distraction count, focus predictions and ratings, recharge status, and whether a session was a buddy session
  • Tasks you create, including titles, completion status, and accumulated focus time
  • App preferences such as timer durations, theme, sound settings, notification preferences, and your display name for buddy sessions
  • Wake-up voice recordings you create for break-end alerts (stored only on your device, never uploaded)
  • Break recharge data derived from motion sensors during break periods (only the aggregate percentage is stored)

👥 Buddy Session Data

When you use the Buddy Session feature to focus with another person, the following information is temporarily stored on Firebase (Google) servers:

  • Display name you choose to enter
  • Task title you choose to share
  • A randomly generated device identifier (not linked to your Apple ID or personal identity)
  • Session timing data including start time, duration, and completion status
  • Focus status such as whether you are focused, away, or supporting your buddy
  • Distraction metrics including the number of times you left the app and total time away
  • Buddy ratings (1-5 stars) if you choose to rate your session partner

This data is automatically deleted from the servers within 24 hours of the session ending.

💳 Subscription Data

When you subscribe to FocusHaven Pro, your purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. RevenueCat is used to manage subscription status and receives your purchase receipt from Apple for verification. Your payment information, Apple ID, and billing details are never collected by FocusHaven. Please refer to RevenueCat's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy for details.

🚫 Information Not Collected

  • No real name, email address, phone number, or account credentials are required or collected (there are no user accounts)
  • No location data is collected
  • No access to your camera, contacts, calendar, or health data
  • No advertising identifiers (IDFA) are used
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs are used
  • No advertisements are served

⚙️ How We Use Your Information

  • On-device data is used solely to provide app functionality (timers, task tracking, statistics, and personalization) and is synced only within your own iCloud account
  • Buddy session data is used solely to enable real-time collaborative focus sessions between you and your session partner
  • Subscription data is used solely to verify your subscription status and unlock premium features

🔐 Device Permissions

The App requests the following permissions, each used only for the stated purpose:

  • Microphone: To record personal wake-up voice messages that play when your break ends. Recordings are stored only on your device and are never uploaded.
  • Motion & Fitness: To detect physical activity during break periods and calculate a "recharge percentage." Raw sensor data is processed on-device in real time and is not stored or transmitted.
  • Notifications: To alert you when your focus or break timer completes and when events occur in buddy sessions.

☁️ iCloud Sync

Your tasks and focus session history are synced across your devices using Apple's CloudKit service. This data is stored in your personal iCloud account and is not accessible to the developer. You can manage or delete this data through your device's iCloud settings.

🗄️ Data Retention

  • On-device data persists until you delete the app or clear app data
  • iCloud data persists until you delete it from iCloud settings or delete the app
  • Buddy session data is automatically deleted from Firebase within 24 hours
  • Subscription records are retained by Apple and RevenueCat per their respective policies

🗑️ Data Deletion

You can delete your data in the following ways:

  • Wake-up voice recordings: Delete individual recordings in Settings > Wake-Up Voice
  • Tasks and session history: Delete the app to remove all local data, or manage iCloud storage in Settings > Apple ID > iCloud
  • Buddy session data: Leave or cancel a session to remove your participant data; all session data is auto-deleted within 24 hours
  • All preferences and local data: Uninstall and reinstall the app to reset everything

Since there are no user accounts, there is no account deletion process needed. All locally stored data is removed when you delete the app.

👶 Children's Privacy

FocusHaven is suitable for users of all ages (rated 4+ on the App Store). The App does not require any personal information to function. No account creation, email, or real name is required. No personally identifiable information is knowingly collected from any user, including children.

📝 Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top. You are encouraged to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
