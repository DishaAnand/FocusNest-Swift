Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 18, 2026

Disha Anand ("we," "us," or "our") built FocusHaven as a productivity application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use FocusHaven ("the App").

Information We Collect

Information Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device and synced across your own devices via your personal iCloud account. We do not have access to this data.

  • Focus session history including duration, completion status, distraction count, and focus ratings
  • Tasks you create, including titles and completion status
  • App preferences such as timer durations, theme, sound settings, and notification preferences
  • Wake-up voice recordings you create for break-end alerts (stored only on your device, never uploaded)
  • Break recharge data derived from motion sensors during break periods (only the aggregate percentage is stored)

Information Collected for Buddy Sessions

When you use the Buddy Session feature to focus with another person, the following information is temporarily stored on our servers (Firebase by Google):

  • Display name you choose to enter
  • Task title you choose to share
  • A randomly generated device identifier (not linked to your Apple ID or personal identity)
  • Session timing data including start time, duration, and completion status
  • Focus status such as whether you are focused, away, or supporting your buddy
  • Distraction metrics including the number of times you left the app and total time away
  • Buddy ratings (1-5 stars) if you choose to rate your session partner

This data is automatically deleted from our servers within 24 hours of the session ending.

Information Collected for Subscriptions

When you subscribe to FocusHaven Pro, your purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status. RevenueCat receives your purchase receipt from Apple to verify your subscription. We do not collect your payment information, Apple ID, or billing details. Please refer to RevenueCat's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy for details on how they handle purchase data.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any account credentials (there are no user accounts)
  • We do not collect your location
  • We do not access your camera, contacts, calendar, or health data
  • We do not use advertising identifiers (IDFA)
  • We do not use any analytics or tracking SDKs
  • We do not serve advertisements

How We Use Your Information

  • On-device data is used solely to provide app functionality (timers, task tracking, statistics, and personalization) and is synced only within your own iCloud account
  • Buddy session data is used solely to enable real-time collaborative focus sessions between you and your session partner
  • Subscription data is used solely to verify your subscription status and unlock premium features

Device Permissions

The App requests the following permissions, each used only for the stated purpose:

  • Microphone: To record personal wake-up voice messages that play when your break ends. Recordings are stored only on your device and are never uploaded to any server.
  • Motion & Fitness: To detect physical activity during break periods and calculate a "recharge percentage." Raw sensor data is processed on-device in real time and is not stored or transmitted. Only the aggregate recharge percentage is saved locally.
  • Notifications: To alert you when your focus or break timer completes and when events occur in buddy sessions.

iCloud Sync

Your tasks and focus session history are synced across your devices using Apple's CloudKit service. This data is stored in your personal iCloud account and is not accessible to us. You can manage or delete this data through your device's iCloud settings.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

Data Retention

  • On-device data persists until you delete the app or clear app data
  • iCloud data persists until you delete it from iCloud settings or delete the app
  • Buddy session data is automatically deleted from Firebase within 24 hours
  • Subscription records are retained by Apple and RevenueCat per their respective policies

Data Deletion

You can delete your data in the following ways:

  • Wake-up voice recordings: Delete individual recordings in Settings > Wake-Up Voice
  • Tasks and session history: Delete the app to remove all local data, or manage iCloud storage in your device's Settings > Apple ID > iCloud
  • Buddy session data: Leave or cancel a session to remove your participant data; all session data is auto-deleted within 24 hours regardless
  • All preferences and local data: Uninstall and reinstall the app to reset everything

Since there are no user accounts, there is no account deletion process needed. All locally stored data is removed when you delete the app.

Children's Privacy

FocusHaven does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. The App does not require any personal information to function, and buddy session participation requires only a voluntarily chosen display name.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
